# Kennedi Workbook: Replace Locked Pose 04-helping (Canonical Puppy)

Owner-directed art fix following a character-consistency audit of the eight
locked Boss Kennedi poses (2026-09-23), scored against the "Locked identity
traits" list in `workbook/docs/design-system.md`.

## Before Code

### Root Cause

`workbook/design-source/boss-kennedi/locked-poses/04-helping.{png,svg}` --
page 6's hero scene and its correct-answer picture card -- fails 6 of the 10
locked identity traits: it reads as an older child (longer face, smaller
almond eyes), hair is worn down with one tie instead of two pigtails, and she
wears puffy shorts with no socks and no shoes. The other seven poses hold the
traits (identity >=3/4 against `01-neutral` in 6 of 7).

Cause, confirmed by reproduction: the prompt embedded in the locked PNG's
metadata, re-run with its recorded seed (2845242026) and Redux strength
(0.08), regenerates the locked PNG pixel-identically. That prompt never
mentions socks, shoes, face shape, eyes or age, and omits both guard phrases
the design doc requires in every prompt ("bold thick solid black outlines
(not thin or faint)" and "no letters, no numbers"). The puppy in the image
is also not the canonical puppy (`animals/locked-poses/01-sitting`).

Replacement art (selected by the owner from 15 generated candidates, then
repaired): candidate "B" -- same generator (`tools/comfy-generate-redux.py`,
unmodified), trait-complete prompt, Redux 0.08, seed 202 -- with two
FLUX.1 Fill inpaint repairs composited back through their masks (a stray
tail removed; a six-digit hand corrected to five), the canonical locked puppy
composited in front of her at matched line weight, and leftover strokes of
the replaced dog removed. Known deviations the owner accepted: puffy shorts
instead of the pleated skirt, and a small rectangular badge instead of the
round star badge.
