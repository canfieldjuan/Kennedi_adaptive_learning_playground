## Behaviour

- `recolor-kennedi-skin.py SPEC.json` reads the source PNG and the palette
  that the spec names, and writes the output PNG it names.
  - **Mask:**
    - flat skin, by the spec's gates;
    - skin shading connected to it without crossing an outline, kept only
      where a region is at least half flat skin;
    - a 1 px edge into the outline;
    - nothing below the spec's ground row.
  - **Skin and shading:** the palette skin times (pixel luminance / source
    skin luminance). Shadows come out darker golden brown, not salmon.
  - **Cheeks:** the source blush (hue at most 14 degrees, in full light)
    and its darker rim are flattened to the skin, then blended toward the
    palette blush by a weight softened over 4 px, at the palette's
    strength.
  - Every other pixel is byte-identical to the source.
- `--check` recomputes the output and compares its pixels with the
  committed file. Any difference exits 1. Pixels, not bytes, because PNG
  encoders on different machines may write the same pixels differently.
- **Invariants:** the output is deterministic; pixels outside the mask are
  unchanged; the output's size and mode equal the source's.
- **Failure cases**, each exiting with a message before writing anything:
  - a missing source, palette or spec;
  - a spec without a required key;
  - an output path equal to the source.

## Contract Amendments

- **Root cause corrected before any code** (2026-10-04). The first version
  quoted the provenance entry's "seed not recorded" as the reason the cover
  can't be re-rendered. Reading the PNG showed its seed is embedded. The
  reason is now the true one: a changed prompt redraws the image. Scope
  gains one line, already inside the provenance item: the cover's entry
  records its seed.
- **The tool needs only numpy and Pillow.** The workbook gate's Python has
  no scipy. So the 4-connected region labelling and the cross-shaped
  dilation the preview took from `scipy.ndimage` are written out in the
  tool. Its output must equal the approved preview's pixels.
- **The art-direction proof page leaves scope.**
  `src/content/proof-pages/page-01-cover-art-proof.mjs` renders to
  `dist-proof/`, which isn't part of the book. Its committed screenshot
  came from a separate manual step. Repointing its source without that
  rebuild would leave the committed proof disagreeing with its source.
  It stays a historical record of the original integration proof:
  `page-01-cover-art-proof.mjs` and `dist-proof/` move to Must Not Change.
  Only `page-01-cover.mjs` (the book's page 1) points at the recolored
  cover.

  - `:26-33`: the structural constants (minimum region, skin share, edge
